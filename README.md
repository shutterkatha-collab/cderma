## Design System: Clinical Botanical Luxury

The project's design system is documented in [`DESIGN.md`](file:///Users/shutter_katha/Downloads/Old/c-derma/DESIGN.md).

### Core Palette
- **Primary (`#1E2322`)**: Deep Graphite
- **Secondary (`#3D5A50`)**: Deep Botanical Sage
- **Tertiary (`#5A7B6F`)**: Muted Alpine Green
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF9F6`)**: Warm Alabaster / Ivory
- **Clinical Surface (`#F4F6F4`)**: Soft Clinical Sage-Grey
- **Mineral Accent (`#EBF1F2`)**: Clinical Glacial Blue

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Playfair Display (Serif)
- **Body & Technical Specs**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-serif)
- **Clinical Badges & Metadata**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Caps, tracked `0.14em - 0.18em`)
