Brand Story

About cDerma

Science-Led Skincare. Professional Approach.

cDerma is a dermatology-focused skincare brand by K&K Trading Concern, created to provide thoughtful, science-led skincare solutions for common and challenging skin concerns.

Our formulations are developed with a strong focus on active ingredients, skin compatibility and practical dermatological care. We aim to provide products that can complement the skincare approach of dermatologists and other healthcare professionals while remaining simple and understandable for consumers.

From acne and pigmentation to sensitive skin, hydration, skin-barrier support and sun protection, cDerma focuses on solutions designed around real skincare needs.

We believe strong dermatological skincare is built on science, quality, transparency and responsible formulation. Our goal is to continuously improve our products through current ingredient knowledge, professional feedback and evolving skincare science.

As cDerma grows in Nepal, we aim to build meaningful relationships with dermatologists, healthcare professionals, clinics and pharmacies, while keeping better skin health at the centre of everything we do.
